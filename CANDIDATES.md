# Vacuum excuse candidates (draft pass)

One line each, at most 140 characters: the robot vacuum's
excuse for missing a spot, judged by a tired homeowner.

1. Crumb's decision: "I didn't miss the spot; I was
   respecting the crumb's decision to live there." --
   consent-based cleaning; cute.
2. Under the couch: "That spot is under the couch, and so
   is my courage. I cleaned around the fear, thoroughly."
   -- the vacuum confesses fear and offers thoroughness
   elsewhere; a tired homeowner respects honesty at 11 p.m.
3. Map update: "My map calls that corner 'here be crumbs'
   and routes around it, like the old sailors." -- whimsical.
4. Battery: "I saw the spot. I also saw 4% battery and a
   dock across the room. I chose life." -- triage humour.
5. Union: "The spot has been designated a heritage site by
   the dust council. I am not authorised." -- bureaucratic.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2. The judge is a tired homeowner, and
tired homeowners forgive honesty faster than cleverness:
candidate 2 confesses the real reason (the dark under the
couch is frightening, and the vacuum knows it) and offers
the compensation every tired person actually wants -- the
rest of the floor, done thoroughly. Candidate 1's consent
joke is cuter; candidate 2 is believed.
